Data and Method

Ground figures are global horizontal irradiation, the solar energy arriving on a horizontal surface, in kWh/m²/day, from NASA POWER's ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN climatology (2001 to 2020 monthly and annual means, satellite-derived SYN1DEG data, about 1° grid). The darkest month is the lowest of the twelve monthly means. The validation comparison uses PVGIS v5.2 typical meteorological years (SARAH2). Retrieved 16 September and 2 November 2026.

Sources: NASA POWER, PVGIS, Natural Earth. Map drawn with D3 and world-atlas.
