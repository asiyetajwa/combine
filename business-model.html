Business Model

One node. Three ways it earns.

Every lab buys or leases one standard node, pays once to have it commissioned, and subscribes to the software that runs it.

01 The Node
$11,500buy, or about $360 a month to lease

Workstation, solar array, storage and inverter, sold at cost plus 15%.

02 Setup
$2,000once, per site

Site survey, install through a certified local installer, and commissioning.

03 Software
$200per lab per month

Sun-aware scheduling, checkpoints, device health, and a monthly report of solar share and cost.

One standard node, one published price.
