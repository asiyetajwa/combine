# Combine Clips

Four clips generated on Vertex AI (Veo 3.1) on 2026-11-03 from prompt pack v2.
Two takes per shot; the best passing take per shot was selected after a
frame check. Each clip is 8 seconds, 1920x1080, with audio.

## Shots

- clip-0.mp4: hero cabinet, golden hour solar node in open grassland (take 1)
- clip-1.mp4: lab workstation, developer in a small independent AI studio (take 1)
- clip-2.mp4: yard unit, camera pole node overlooking a logistics yard (take 2)
- clip-3.mp4: data center, aerial solar field then a server hall with one engineer (take 3)

## Provenance (v2)

Prompt pack v2, rendered as ingredients from the approved hardware renders
(hw-cabinet, hw-workstation, hw-yard, hw-family), not from earlier logo or
cabinet reference images. Shot 3 was regenerated from the same pack for
consistency; earlier takes of shot 3 were rejected in the frame check (see below).

## Mark-only rules (these govern the v2 clips)

- Enclosures are very dark forest-green anodised aluminium, matte bead-blasted
  finish, softly chamfered edges, graphite accents, dark recessed vents.
- The only branding is a small C-shaped mark laser-etched into the metal in
  the same dark green, slightly satin against the matte surface, with a tiny
  diamond beside it that glows soft leaf green as a status light.
- No plates, no labels, no stickers, no tiles, no white or cream enclosures,
  no text of any kind on the equipment.
- Frame check: any take showing text, letters, a logo plate/tile/sticker, or
  a white/cream enclosure was rejected. Shot 3 takes 1 and 2 were rejected
  (a garbled word appeared under the etched mark in the interior frames);
  replacement takes were generated for shot 3 only and re-checked before
  this push.
- Brian's standing rule for the edit: it carries no such label text anywhere.
