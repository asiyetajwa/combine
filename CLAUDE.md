# Combine: owner rules for every session

Combine is solar-assisted compute for small AI labs, a Circle+ company. These rules come from Brian (owner) and apply to the site, mockups, decks, exports, emails and films.

## Brand

- Combine is identified by its mark only: the C with the diamond. Never set the word "combine" next to the mark, on hardware, in films or as a lockup.
- Hardware (approved 3 November 2026): very dark forest-green anodised aluminium, the C etched tone on tone, the diamond as a leaf-green status light. No plates, tiles, stickers or labels. Reference renders and the Veo prompt pack live on the agent bridge in `shared/combine-veo-v2-20261103/`.
- Never use the font **Instrument Serif** (roman or italic), anywhere. Owner blacklist, 2026-11-03.
- No em dashes in any copy. No diagonal arrows on links. Title Case for navigation, links and calls to action.

## Voice on the site

- Present Combine as a confident product. The landing pages do not argue against themselves: no disclaimer banners, no "not a measurement", "illustrative", "conceptual only" or "no labs use Combine" notes, no "concept render" labels.
- The only place the site may say something is a concept or simulation is the chip (its film and the Inside the Chip section).
- The project's full status (stage, pilots, renders, what is measured and what is modelled) lives in the founder's letter on the Founder page, signed with Brian's signature.
- Never invent customers, pilots, partners, testimonials or measured results. A figure that comes from the energy model is labelled "modelled".
- No "Concept film" text in any video.
